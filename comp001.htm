    • What dose RioTinto mine

    Iron ore Iron ore is the core of Rio Tinto’s Western Australian operations, centred in the Pilbara.

    Salt Rio Tinto produces industrial salt at Dampier Salt in the Pilbara Dampier Salt is one of the world’s largest solar salt operations.

    Lithium Rio Tinto produces lithium at Mt Cattlin, near Ravensthorpe in southern Western Australia Lithium is a growing part of the company’s WA portfolio due to battery‑materials demand.

    Copper–gold (development/exploration) Rio Tinto is advancing Winu, a copper‑gold project in the Pilbara Winu is not yet a full production mine but is a significant future resource as development advances.
