Introduction to Mining in Western Australia

Mining is one of Western Australias most important industries, driving a significant portion of the states economy and exports. The region is rich in mineral resources, with major commodities including iron ore, gold, bauxite/alumina, nickel, and lithium.

The Economic Importance of Mining in WA

Some of the key minerals mined in Western Australia include iron ore, gold, nickel, and lithium. The state is home to some of the world's largest mining operations, attracting investment from both domestic and international companies.

Environmental Impact

Mining plays a major role in Western Australias economy, but it also leaves a significant environmental footprint. Large-scale land clearing, excavation, and waste disposal often lead to habitat loss, soil degradation, and water contamination. Sensitive ecosystems, including those on Indigenous lands, can be disrupted, affecting biodiversity and cultural heritage.
